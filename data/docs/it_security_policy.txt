IT Security Policy (all staff)

Passwords must be at least 12 characters and are rotated every 180 days. Never share passwords or paste them into chats or AI tools.
Laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.
Remote access to internal systems requires the corporate VPN and multi-factor authentication.
Customer personal data (Aadhaar, PAN, card numbers) must not be sent to external AI services; use the approved ShieldGate gateway which tokenizes such values.
Report suspected incidents to security@company.example within one hour.

Leave Policy (all staff)

Every employee receives 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly at 1.5 days.
Sick leave: 8 days per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 2 consecutive days.
Casual leave: 6 days per year, to be applied for at least one working day in advance where possible.

Carry-forward: up to 10 days of unused annual leave can be carried into the next year. Casual and sick leave do not carry forward.
Interns receive 1 day of leave per month of service and are not eligible for carry-forward.

To apply, submit the request in the HR portal. The reporting manager approves or declines within two working days.
